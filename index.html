About me

I’m a computer science undergraduate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

I’m interested in site reliability engineering, AI agents, and applied AI. My work focuses on building AI systems and models that help reliability teams resolve failures faster, and on measuring whether they can be trusted on live systems.

I do research on AI for site reliability engineering in Professor Tianyin Xu’s SREGym group, and this past summer I worked in the same area as an AI Algorithm Intern at ByteDance. I’m also on the course staff of CS 233 Computer Architecture. I expect to graduate in May 2027.
