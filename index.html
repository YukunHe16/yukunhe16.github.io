About me

I’m a computer science undergraduate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

I’m interested in site reliability engineering, AI agents, and applied AI. My work focuses on connecting agents to real operational workflows and evaluating how well they work.

I recently became a contributor to SREGym, where I explore how agents investigate and diagnose infrastructure failures. I expect to graduate with a B.S. in Computer Science in May 2027.
